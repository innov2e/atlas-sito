Understand how a Caspio Bridge app is built before you change it.

INNOV2E Atlas reads the Export of your app and gives you documentation for every object, a map of what depends on what, and a list of what is no longer needed.

macOS 12 or later, Mac with Apple Silicon.

What you find inside the Export

An app grows for years, changes hands, and collects copies and experiments. Atlas reads all of it and tells you what is there, what is connected and what you can remove.

  • Documentation

    Tables, fields, views, DataPages, authentications, triggered actions and scheduled tasks: one page per object, exported as HTML or Markdown.

  • The map

    A 2D and 3D graph of dependencies. Pick an object and see what uses it and what breaks if you change it.

  • Issues

    Fields never used or always empty, orphan tables and views, backup copies, disabled triggers, expired tasks, broken references.

The Export stays on your Mac

Atlas works locally. It has no account, collects no usage statistics, and sends neither the Export nor your Project data anywhere.

  • It works offline.
  • It goes online only to look for an update, and only when you ask: with Check now, or by turning on Check at startup, which is off by default.
  • In that case it contacts atlas.innov2e.it to see whether a new version exists and github.com to download it. Every package is verified with a signature before it is installed.
  • The app is signed and notarized by Apple.
